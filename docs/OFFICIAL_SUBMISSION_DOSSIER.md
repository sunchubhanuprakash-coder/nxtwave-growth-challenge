### 1. What changed between your first idea and final solution?
* **First Idea:** My initial thought was to distribute the ?2,000 budget across targeted Instagram/Facebook ads and send cold bulk emails to engineering students.

* **Final Solution:** I abandoned paid digital ads entirely and pivoted to a **zero-paid-ad, organic compounding model**. * **AI Suggestion #3: "Use an external Large Language Model (OpenAI API) for all live predictions and recommendations."**
